# Game Design

## Core Loop

Move, survive enemy waves, collect experience gems, level up, and choose upgrades.

## Player

- Moves with WASD.
- Gains experience from dropped gems.
- Levels up when experience reaches the current threshold.

## Combat

- Fireball automatically targets the nearest enemy.
- Spinning swords orbit the player and damage enemies on contact.

## Progression

- Each level-up pauses the game and presents three random upgrade options.
- Selecting one option applies it and resumes the game.
